Privacy Policy

ABC Learning app

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ABC Learning is a learning app for children and teenagers, from babies to Year 12. We built it so families can use it safely. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, photos, contacts or microphone.

What stays on your device

Results, best scores, the feelings journal and settings (like read-aloud on/off) are saved only on your own device so that children and parents can look back at them. This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or using "Clear all results" in the app) removes it.

Read-aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech voice built into your device. The words are spoken by your device; we do not record or receive any audio.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, the app is suitable for children of all ages and is designed to comply with children's privacy laws, including the Australian Privacy Act, COPPA (USA) and GDPR-K (EU/UK).

Third parties

The app works fully offline and does not contain third-party advertising or analytics code. It does not link out to other websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
