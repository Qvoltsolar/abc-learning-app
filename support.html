Common questions

Read-aloud isn't working. Turn up the volume, make sure the device isn't on silent, and check the 🔊 button at the top of the app is on. On Android, make sure a text-to-speech voice is installed (Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech).

Where are my results saved? Only on your device. They aren't backed up to the cloud, so they won't move to a new device.

Does the app need internet? No. Everything works offline.

Is it safe for kids? Yes – no ads, no accounts, no chat, no data collection. See our privacy policy.

Feeling worried or upset? Kids and teens in Australia can call Kids Helpline on 1800 55 1800 – free, any time.
